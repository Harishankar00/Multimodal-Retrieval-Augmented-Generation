# **Project Title:** 

# **Image-Based / Multimodal Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) for Visual Question Answering (VQA)** 

|**Task**|**Description**|
|---|---|
|**Objective**|Develop an AI application that accepts an image as input and answers<br>user questions related to the image using Multimodal RAG.|
|**Project Overview**|Image-based or Multimodal Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)<br>extends traditional text RAG by enabling systems to ingest, retrieve,<br>and reason over visual data such as charts, diagrams, documents,<br>receipts, and photographs alongside text.|
|**Input**|Images (JPG, PNG, JPEG) or PDF documents|
|**Output**|Accurate answers based on the visual content and extracted text.|
|**Core**<br>**Functionality**|Understand objects, text, layouts, charts, and diagrams within an image<br>and answer user queries in natural language.|
|**Example**<br>**Questions**|What is shown in the image?<br>• What is the total amount on the receipt?<br>• How many people are present?<br>• Summarize this document.<br>• What does the graph indicate?<br>• What is written on the signboard?|
|**OCR**|PaddleOCR or EasyOCR|
|**Vector Database**|FAISS or ChromaDB|
|**Frontend**|Streamlit / React|
|**Backend**|FastAPI / Flask|
